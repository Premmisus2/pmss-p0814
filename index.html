Why this matters more for you

A no-show costs a shop barber a chair. It costs you the chair and the drive.

That is the whole difference between Driva and a barbershop with a door. A shop fills the gap with a walk-in. You cannot. You have already spent the fuel and the time getting there, and the slot behind it is now too tight to sell. Reminders, confirmations and a deposit at the time of booking are not nice extras for a mobile barber. They are the business.

What we would build

Five pieces. Built once.

You own all of it. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours and it keeps running.

A website built around one button

Not a brochure. A page whose only job is to turn someone who heard about you into a booked appointment, with your service area, your prices and your work on it.

Online booking that runs without you

They pick a slot, it confirms itself, and it texts them a reminder before you drive over. They can reschedule themselves instead of leaving you guessing.

A Google profile built for a business with no address

Google supports mobile businesses properly. You set a service radius instead of a storefront, and you start appearing when someone nearby searches for a barber who comes to them. Right now that search does not find you at all.

Reviews asked for automatically

Every client gets asked after their cut, without you remembering to ask. Every customer, no filtering, which is both the honest way and the only way Google allows.

One link, and a code for the van

Instagram bio, Facebook, WhatsApp and a QR code on the van all point to the same booking page. It replaces the cards you have run out of, and it never runs out.
